# Mandatory Assignment: AI Agents ("AI Agent Challenge")

Source: `AIAgentOpgave_ItaML_6sem.pdf` (5 pages), ITA ML 6th semester, dated 5 Oct. 2026, author Sila. Original is Danish; this is a faithful English rendering with key terms kept.

## Hand-in rules

- **Group assignment, 2-3 members** (fewer or more not allowed).
- The group delivers a **3-5 minute video** documenting their work on an IT solution where AI agents play a central role.
- Canvas hand-in: **one PDF per group** with project title, **full names of all members**, and a **link to the YouTube video**. May include very short code fragments/explanations, but keep it very short: **absolute max 2 pages**.
- **Deadline: "torsdag d. 2 November" kl. 20.00** on Canvas. Note [sic]: 2 Nov 2026 is a Monday, and the sibling Titanic/Supply Chain assignment says Monday 2 November.
- Expected effort: just under a day, **6-7 hours**, of the autumn mandatory assignment work.
- YouTube video may be Unlisted; the group must check the link works and plays without requesting access.

## Purpose

Develop an idea and prototype for an IT solution where AI agents play a central role. Identify a real problem where an AI agent creates value for a user, company, organisation or other target group. **The solution must be more than a traditional chatbot.**

The agent should, to a relevant extent, be able to:

- access and use information from data sources, APIs, databases or other systems
- understand the context it works in
- carry out multiple steps to solve a task
- use tools or external services
- retain relevant information/state when needed
- turn information and reasoning into a concrete, useful action or result

Not everything must be fully implemented, but the group must investigate how it could be solved and what is needed to make the agent work in practice.

## Three guiding questions (from idea to working agent)

1. **Which problem will you solve?** Describe the user/target group, the problem, why an AI agent makes sense, and what real value the agent creates.
2. **How should your AI agent work?** Architecture and technologies (LLMs, APIs, databases, RAG, memory, tools/function calling, web services, ...). Which actions the agent itself should perform.
3. **How far can you get?** Realistic plan: what after the first hours, what after one working day. Prioritise a small working solution over a big idea that is never tested.

Not expected: a finished product. Expected: experiment, implement, test, learn from what works and what does not.

- Organised by GenAI.Works, online **15-20 October 2026** (https://hackathon.genai.works/event/open-agent-hackathon-2026).
- Falls in the autumn break, so participation is not expected. Originally students were to register, but site access has been unstable, so it is now only inspiration material.

## Video requirements (min 3, max 5 minutes)

- Present the problem you worked on and your solution idea.
- Show and explain your AI agent and the main technologies used (narrate and use screen capture).
- Say how far you got.
- Highlight key lessons: what did you learn, what worked, what was difficult, what would you continue with.
- A finished or flawless solution is not required; the video documents work, results and learning.

## Context for later

This is the first step in the rest of the semester: later you get an **exam assignment** where you can further develop, change or extend this solution and experience. The work is professional ballast to build on.

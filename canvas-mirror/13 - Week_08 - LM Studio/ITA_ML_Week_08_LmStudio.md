Source: `ITA_ML_Week_08_LmStudio.pdf`. Mostly diagrams and figures from "AI Agents in Action" by Michael Lanham; the text is only slide titles. Not viewed as images.

- Running LLMs in LM Studio; prompt engineering.
- More agents working on a task.
- Agents calling functions; the behaviour tree calling pattern.
- Prompting the LM Studio server.
- Exercises 1-6 (see `Exercises_Week_08_LmStudio.md`).

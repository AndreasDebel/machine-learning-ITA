# Exercise Chatbase (Week 8 - AI Agents, group exercise)

Source: `Exercise_Chatbase.pdf` (Danish; the PDF header says "Exercises Week 7 - Ai Agents"). Groups of 2-3 (preferably 2). Free exercise: do a small investigation on your own, then give a 2-3 min presentation (about 3 slides, or one slide plus a demo). About one hour; plan the time yourselves.

## Task
- Investigate how to integrate an AI agent into your own code. Imagine an existing product that you want to add an AI agent to. Keep it as easy as possible.
- First survey what options exist on the internet. The survey may be part of the presentation.
- Then go deeper and try as much as time allows; take notes along the way (the notes are the mini-presentation).
- Unless you already have something of your own to show, work with Chatbase (see the slides, `Build a chatbot.pdf`). Follow the slide steps, explore from there, and note things for the presentation. If you use something else, explore that site or technology in the same way.
- Links: https://www.chatbase.co/ (platform for building and deploying AI support agents), YouTube walkthrough https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z_Ld8Q5zoUk

## Teacher's example
A document about the IT architect programme was uploaded so the bot can answer questions from it (https://www.chatbase.co/tKIJB_e54RWAk5mtcsVuZ/help). What remains is integrating it into an app, for example an alumni-activities app for IT architect students. The presentation covers what was found about integration and thoughts on how it would work. Students do a similar mini-investigation.

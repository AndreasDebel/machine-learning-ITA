# ML Exercises, day 7 - Exercise Comedians, Decision Tree

Source: `ComediansDecisionTree.pdf` (2 pages). In groups of 2-3. Header says "day 7"; "Available February 19th 2024".

Scenario: decide whether to go to a standup show. A person registered, for every comedy show in town, information about the comedian and whether he/she went. From that a decision tree is built that uses earlier decisions to calculate the odds of wanting to see a comedian.

Tasks:
1. Look at the code `Comedians.py`. Change it to show different sizes/depths of the decision tree. In the group, interpret what the tree shows.
2. Use the tree to predict: should I see a show starring a 40-year-old American comedian, with 10 years of experience, and a comedy ranking of 7?
3. What would the answer be if the comedy rank were 6?

References: https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_ml_decision_tree.asp and https://www.kaggle.com/code/mruanova/decision-tree-for-comedy-shows-w3schools

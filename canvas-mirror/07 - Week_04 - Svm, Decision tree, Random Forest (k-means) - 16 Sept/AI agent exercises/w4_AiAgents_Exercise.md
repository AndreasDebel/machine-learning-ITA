# ML Exercise, Agents in IT solutions. Week 4

Source: `w4_AiAgents_Exercise.pdf` (2 pages), ITA ML.

Format: groups of 2-3. Discuss in group. No code. Write (very shortly) answers to exercises 2, 3 and 4 in the template doc (Canvas) and send it to the teacher at the end. 7 minutes per exercise.

## Exercise 1
What is an AI-agent? Talk this through in the group (ask a chatbot).

## Exercise 2 - What is your solution about?
Design an IT solution with AI agents. Imagine your group has been asked to develop a new IT solution in which one or more AI agents play an important role. It may address education, business, healthcare, public services, everyday life, entertainment or another area. You are not expected to build it, only to discuss what the team could realistically create.
- What problem does the solution solve, and who would use it?
- What do the AI agent(s) actually do? Why is an agent useful here instead of a traditional IT system?

## Exercise 3 - What technologies would you need?
Make a simple overview of technologies. Consider: AI Agent, LLM, Tools/APIs, Data/Database, User/Application. Discuss what is needed for:
- the AI agent and its reasoning/decision-making
- an LLM
- external tools or APIs
- databases or other data sources
- backend and frontend
- communication between components

Exact technology choices are not required; explain what the parts must do (be brief, 7 minutes).

## Exercise 4 - How far could your group realistically get?
Imagine starting development today. What could be achieved after:
- 1 day: what could work? Could you demonstrate one small part of the idea?
- 3 days: what could a first prototype contain? Could an agent already interact with a tool, API or database?
- 1 week: what would a realistic MVP look like? What could you demonstrate to another group?

# Logistic Regression, and ChatGPT

Source: `Exercises/Exercise_LogReg_ChatGPT.pdf`, 1 page (page image viewed: text plus Erhvervsakademi Aarhus logo, no figures), Sila, September 1st, 2023. Work in groups of 2-3.

- **Exercise A.** Ask ChatGPT what Logistic Regression is, and have it explain in a simple way. Discuss the answer in the group; make sure everyone understands it.
- **Exercise B.** After you have trained your model, how can it be used to decide where a new point belongs? How is this calculated? Let ChatGPT explain, then discuss its answer.
- **Exercise C.** Ask ChatGPT for at least 5 examples of where logistic regression is used, and have it explain how, if you are uncertain about the use.
